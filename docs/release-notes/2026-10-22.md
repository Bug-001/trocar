# 2026-10-22

## Read failures on the board say what could not be read

### Board

- **When the board cannot read something, it says what and offers Retry.** On the overview, the traffic pages,
  Settings, the session list and the Capture log, a failed read now shows one plain sentence: what could not be read
  and whether the trocar service could not be reached or answered with an error code, with **Retry**. The server's
  own error text (for example a crashed worker's trace) is only under **Technical details**.
