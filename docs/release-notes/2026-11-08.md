# 2026-11-08

## Uninstall

- **`trocar uninstall` no longer says it stopped a collector that had already exited.** When the collector had been
  ended by hand (for example, on Windows, by killing its supervisor), its lock still named its pid, and the receipt
  said "The collector (pid …) was stopped." though uninstall had stopped nothing. It now says nothing about a
  collector, as it does when none was there.
