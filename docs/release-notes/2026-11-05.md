# 2026-11-05

## The board

- **Moving around a session that is still being read keeps up with you.** Reading a growing session used to run in
  a long chain of small jobs that kept the next frame waiting, so a step change could take two or three times as
  long whenever a piece had just arrived. The desk now lets your key press or click reach the screen first and
  redraws what arrived a moment later; the side panels and the call drawer follow once you stop.
- **A click on a lane tick is no longer lost.** If new data redrew the lanes between pressing and releasing the mouse,
  the browser dropped the click and the step stayed where it was. The lanes now wait for you to let go.
- **Opening a step lands where it should when its neighbour is still loading.** A step that arrived before the step
  it is compared with showed no new rows, or marked them against an older step, and the window stayed where it was.
  It now marks and lands when that step arrives, unless you have moved the window yourself.
- **The Readable view says it is one.** With the desk's default cell view set to Readable, a context section's header
  still claimed its blocks were verbatim. It now says "Readable view", and goes back to its verbatim claim when you
  return to Raw.
