# 2026-11-06

## The board

- **The replay page has a new layout.** The time axis strip sits on top, the reading column (the step head and the
  context window) below it, and a sidebar on the right with two tabs. What moved:
  - The outline on the left is now the **Overview** tab: one row per chapter, and the current chapter lists its tool
    definitions, system prompt, prompts and sub-agents, plus **Jump to the change ›** when its cache broke.
  - The raw data panel is gone. A call's request parameters, raw reply and transport now sit folded under
    **Call details** after the reply in the context window.
  - The resource list is now the **Resources** tab. A click on a name opens that block in the context window.
  - A button collapses the sidebar to a thin strip. The boundary between the main area and the sidebar, and the one
    between the strip and the reading column, can be dragged; the page remembers them and the open tab, and a
    double-click on a boundary puts it back.
  - On a narrow window: below 1200 px the sidebar becomes a row of tabs at the bottom whose panel rises over the page;
    below 900 px the track names fold into a **Tracks** menu on the strip, with the problem pager and the track
    toggles; below 600 px only the strip and the reading column remain.

  The replay keys and the Raw and Readable views work as before.
