# 2026-11-06

## Capture

- **A `NO_PROXY` of your own is no longer taken for your proxy.** With a `NO_PROXY` in the settings `trocar on`
  writes to (Claude Code's `settings.json`, for one) and no proxy of your own, trocar read that bypass list as the
  proxy to chain through, so the agent's requests failed where they went direct before. Trocar now takes only the
  proxy setting you had, and `trocar status` shows the upstream as direct.
