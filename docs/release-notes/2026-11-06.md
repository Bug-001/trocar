# 2026-11-06

## The board

- **The capture state's lamp now means the same thing everywhere.** "Capture has problems" lit the red lamp meant for a
  stopped service, and states that only say the board does not know (unreadable, unknown, not refreshed) lit an amber
  one. Now a stopped or silent service is red, problems and leftover changes are amber, capturing shows the green dot,
  and the not-known states light nothing, on the overview card and on the capture page alike.
