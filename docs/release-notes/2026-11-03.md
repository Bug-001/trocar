# 2026-11-03

## The board

- **Each agent now shows its official icon.** Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, Kimi Code, DeepSeek dsh, Hermes, Z-Code
  and pi appear on the board with their own icons instead of drawings made for trocar. An agent trocar does not
  recognise still shows the dashed "unknown agent" mark.
- **The old DeepSeek CLI keeps trocar's own mark.** It appears only in old captures.
- **Quieter dividers, clearer timeline colours.** The replay desk's dividers are now thin lines that light up when
  you point at them or drag them. Every content type in the timeline and its legend has its own clearly different
  colour; images, unrecognised blocks and steps not loaded yet are striped.
- **The board has a new look.** Every screen uses two type families in five sizes, drawn in your system's own
  fonts with nothing downloaded: one for all words, names and numbers (numbers line up in columns), and a
  fixed-width one only for raw bytes and literal identifiers such as paths, hosts, commands and ids. The replay
  desk's timeline sits on a dark screen in both the light and the dark theme. The interface glyphs are trocar's own
  set, and the brand mark is a gradient tile.
- **Smaller fixes to the new look.** The timeline's legend wraps instead of cutting words off, disclosure triangles
  are drawn and sit next to their label, the selected half of a two-way switch stands out in the dark theme, and
  session rows are only as tall as what they show.
- **Times read the same on every screen.** A day is written "Oct 18" (or "Today" / "Yesterday"), a clock time
  "08:00:52", and both together "Oct 18, 08:00:52".
- **One style for words and table heads.** Menus, heads, filters and states use sentence case ("Last active", not
  "Last Active"). Every table head has the same size, weight and colour, and a head with an info glyph sits on the
  same line as the others. Page titles sit at the same height on every page.
- **One status dot.** The capture state shows as one small round dot in its colour, on the overview card and on the
  capture page.
- **Agent icons in the dark theme** all sit on the same tile.
- **The replay step bar** never cuts the prompt number or the time short.
- **Sessions are grouped by day, and the agent comes first.** The session list has one group per day, each headed
  by its date, and the agent's icon and name sit in their own first column. In the collapsed sidebar, a section's
  menu opens after a short hover, or at once when it gets keyboard focus. The replay desk's outline and right page
  start at 236 px and 320 px wide; double-click a divider to return it to that width.
