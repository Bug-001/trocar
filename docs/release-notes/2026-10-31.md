# 2026-10-31

## Linux

- **`sudo trocar setup-helper` says why `setcap` could not run.** When `setcap` was not installed, or not on root's
  PATH, setup-helper stopped with `setcap failed:` and nothing after it. It now says
  `setcap could not run (spawnSync setcap ENOENT); is it installed and on root's PATH?`. A `setcap` that runs and
  refuses still reports its own message.

## Replay desk

- **A long context window no longer stops the page.** Rows and prompt cards off screen are laid out at a preset
  height until they come on screen, and the preset was shorter than the real thing: a closed row by 2 px (more when
  its head wrapped), a folded prompt card by 6 px. In an open card of hundreds of blocks, drawing the rows near the
  visible part and putting back the ones that had left it moved the rows around them, and with some mixes of rows
  the window kept drawing and putting back the same rows without end: the page stopped answering. A row's head and a
  card's head now have one set height, so a closed row or a folded card is the same size on screen and off it, and
  drawing rows in place of the space they held moves nothing.
- **At 600 px and narrower a row's head is two lines.** The role, the tag and the marks are on the first; the
  preview and the size are on the second. Nothing in a head wraps any more: short of room, the preview and then the
  tag are cut short with an ellipsis (hover it for the whole word). The role and the size never shrink; the marks
  shrink only on a two-line head that has no room even for them.
